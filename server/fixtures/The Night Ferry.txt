Chapter 1: The Crossing

The night ferry pushed slowly through the thick fog while the harbor lights faded far behind it, and the old captain stood alone at the wheel, listening to the steady engine hum and the cold dark water slapping softly against the hull.

"Almost there," Ada said.

Chapter 2: The Shore

When the fog finally lifted, the tired passengers crowded along the rail to watch the quiet island rise slowly from the sea, its small white houses glowing gold in the first warm light of the early morning sun far across the bay.
